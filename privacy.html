Privacy Policy

funcwar · Last updated: 2026-10-12

funcwar is a personal tool for publishing the author’s own videos. It does not collect data from other people.

  • What is accessed. Through TikTok Login Kit and the Google OAuth flow the app receives an access token for the author’s own account, only to upload videos.
  • Storage. Tokens are stored locally on the author’s private server with restricted file permissions. They are never shared, sold or published.
  • Third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok and YouTube (Google) for the purpose of publishing the author’s own videos.
  • Revocation. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok and Google account security settings.
